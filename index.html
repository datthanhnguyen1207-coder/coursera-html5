Popular Board Game Categories

Modern tabletop games offer diverse systems that reward planning, resource management, and strategic thinking. Here are three primary genres enjoyed by players worldwide:

  • Eurogames: Focus on resource optimization, engine building, and indirect competition with little to no luck factor.
  • Social Deduction: Emphasize bluffing, communication, and hidden roles among participants.
  • Deck-Building Games: Players draft and construct their personal deck of cards dynamically during play.
